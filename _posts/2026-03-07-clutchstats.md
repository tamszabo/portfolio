*In the spring of 2020, the world stopped, but the 72-10 Chicago Bulls started running again on my screen. I didn't know then that a documentary would reconstruct my entire relationship with the sport.*

---

## The Reignition

I had spent a decade away from the NBA. But as **The Last Dance** unfolded, it wasn't just nostalgia that hit me: it was massive appreciation for the *architecture of a dynasty*.

Watching Michael Jordan - the greatest basketball player of all time - I realized that I still love this game. I came to the conclusion, I couldn't truly know the modern NBA without understanding its foundations. **I had to dig deep into the statistics.**

---

## The Foundation: Why ClutchStats Exists

That hunger to understand the **why** behind the **what** is my day to day job. Whether I'm analyzing a sales funnel for **Hyundai** or a cost structure for **Intrum**, I am looking for the underlying truths.

**ClutchStats** became the vehicle for this obsession. To get familiar with the league again, I didn't just read articles, I scraped team and player data from Basketball Reference. I listened to the "analysts" on ESPN, but I also built bar chart races to visualize the leading scorers of all time.

I spent weeks cleaning and restructuring datasets to ensure every **clutch** moment was backed by the data. I used the same standard I hold myself to in corporate analytics. And through that process, one thing became clear: foundations matter. You can't fully appreciate LeBron James's longevity without understanding the statistical giants who came before him.

---

## An Ode to the Game

This channel is my tribute to a sport that demands excellence. The NBA taught me that **greatness is not an accident. It is a statistical inevitability of hard work and perfect execution.**
